<h1 align="center">Experimental has moved</h1>

To continue recieving updates, either update Experimental through stylus or manually remove Experimental from stylus and install beta [here](https://github.com/tersiswilvin/Roblox-Legacy-Old-Theme/raw/refs/heads/Beta/src/Channels/Beta/RLOT.user.css).

## Why?
Experimental has been split into two branches [Beta](https://github.com/tersiswilvin/Roblox-Legacy-Old-Theme/tree/Beta) & [Dev](https://github.com/tersiswilvin/Roblox-Legacy-Old-Theme/tree/Dev). This is due to Experimental over the years switching over from developer builds to a more stable beta testing system which started making it confusing to existing members on the naming. Due to this realization, it was best decided to split the branch into two with names that should hopefully be less confusing and give people options on a more stable and feature complete but less frequent updated or a build that is less stable and less feature complete but more frequent update.
